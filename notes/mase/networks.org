  Network ID: 192.168.20.0
  Octets: 128 + 64 = 192
  Subnet is = 255.255.255.192
  CIDR has 6 zeroes = 32 - 6 = 26
  Broadcast is the last one 192.168.20.63
  Range is +1 from the network to -1 from the broadcast = 192.168.20.1 - 192.168.20.62
